# Beyond Beauty Spa — Luxury Website Concept

A responsive, editorial-style website concept tailored to **Beyond Beauty Spa, 3779 E Desert Inn Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89121**.

## Preview

Unzip the package and open `index.html` in a modern browser. An internet connection is required for the hosted Google Fonts and Unsplash photography.

## What's improved in this version

* Larger, more readable body copy, navigation, service descriptions and contact details.
* A more visible Beyond Beauty Spa identity: custom BB monogram, full business name and Las Vegas location in the hero.
* The phone number and street address are visible above the fold, with tap-to-call and map directions.
* A dedicated visit card includes the address, phone, map link and hours shown by a public third-party listing.
* More business-specific service information, including hair services named in public booking listings.
* Clear mobile layout and larger touch targets.

## Important before publishing

This is a **private website concept**, not the official website and not an approved representation by the business. The public phone listing used here is **(702) 209-2158** and the listed address is **3779 E Desert Inn Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89121**. Hours and service menus can change; the page explicitly asks visitors to call and confirm. Verify all contact details, current hours, services, prices, booking arrangements and brand permissions with the owner before launch.

The logo is a newly designed typographic concept, not an official supplied logo. Photography is sourced remotely from Unsplash and is illustrative, not photography of the actual spa or its team. Replace it with owner-approved business photography before production.
